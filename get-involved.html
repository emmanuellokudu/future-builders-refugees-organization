Frequently asked questions

Everything partners and community members need to know about FBRO.

Who is FBRO and when was it founded?

Future Builders Refugee Organization (FBRO) is a refugee-led community-based organization founded in 2024 in Kakuma Refugee Camp by refugees responding to community challenges including unemployment, lack of youth opportunities, poverty, conflict, environmental problems, and limited access to education.

Where does FBRO operate?

FBRO operates within Kakuma Refugee Camp and Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement in Turkana County, northwestern Kenya, working with both refugee communities and vulnerable host community members.

Who does FBRO work with in the community?

Our programs serve refugees living in Kakuma, vulnerable host community members, youth and adolescents, women and girls, persons with disabilities, elderly people, and low-income households.

What are FBRO's main program areas?

FBRO manages seven program areas: peacebuilding and social cohesion, livelihood support (crop farming and poultry keeping), youth empowerment and skills development, education and community awareness, environmental conservation, community health and wellbeing, and cultural activities.

How can organizations or donors partner with FBRO?

We invite NGOs, donors, CBOs, government institutions, and local groups to collaborate on practical, community-driven programs. You can fill out the partnership form on this page or email us directly at futurebuildersrefugeeorganizat@gmail.com.
