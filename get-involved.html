Get involved

Partnerships and teamwork help create stronger and more sustainable impact in the community.

Partner with FBRO

FBRO welcomes collaboration with organizations and groups that share our commitment to refugees and vulnerable host communities in Kakuma. Tell us about your organization and the program area you are interested in, and we will reply.

Our work is led by community members, so we look for partners who want to support local leadership and practical, community-driven programs.

We welcome

  • NGOs and humanitarian organizations
  • Refugee-led organizations
  • Community-based organizations (CBOs)
  • Government institutions
  • Donors and development partners
  • Youth and women groups

Take part in our activities

FBRO mobilizes volunteers and community members to take part in peacebuilding, youth empowerment, environmental protection, education, and livelihood activities.

If you would like to ask how to take part, use the form or contact us directly.
