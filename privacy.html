Privacy Policy

How Future Builders Refugee Organization (FBRO) protects personal data, inquiries, and subscriptions.

Last updated: October 2026 | Effective Date: October 12, 2026

1. Overview and Commitment

Future Builders Refugee Organization (FBRO) is a refugee-led community-based organization (CBO) operating in Kakuma Refugee Camp and Kalobeyei Integrated Settlement, Turkana County, Kenya. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you provide when using our website (https://futurebuildersrefugeeorganization.com/).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to share with us:

  • Direct Messages & General Inquiries: When you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional organization name, and the text of your message.
  • Partnership Proposals: When you submit a collaboration request via our Get Involved form, we collect your name, email address, optional organization name, category of inquiry, and message details.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: When you sign up for community progress updates in the footer form, we collect your email address.

3. Purpose of Processing

We use submitted information strictly to:

  • Respond promptly to your questions, feedback, and collaboration proposals.
  • Coordinate humanitarian, peacebuilding, livelihoods, or youth development activities with partners.
  • Send periodic newsletter updates on FBRO initiatives in Kakuma (only if you subscribed).

4. Email and Form Processing (EmailJS & Direct Mail)

Inquiries submitted through our forms are processed securely using client communication services (such as EmailJS) and direct encrypted email transmission (TLS/HTTPS). Form submissions are delivered directly to the designated official organizational inboxes (futurebuildersrefugeeorganizat@gmail.com) and are not retained on public databases.

5. Data Retention

We retain submitted communications only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of your inquiry, maintain active partner communication, or support ongoing community collaboration. If you have subscribed to our newsletter, your email address is retained until you request removal. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing us.

6. No Selling or Commercial Sharing of Personal Data

FBRO does not sell, rent, lease, monetize, or trade your personal data to any commercial third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances. Information is never shared with third parties except when strictly required by law or necessary to execute official community partnerships with your explicit consent.

7. Cookies and Analytics

This website is built with a lightweight, privacy-respecting architecture. We do not use third-party tracking cookies, profiling beacons, or behavioral advertising pixels. Any user interface preferences (such as light or dark display mode) are stored locally on your device within your browser's local storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

8. Your Rights and Contact Information

You have the right to request access to the personal data you have shared with us, request corrections, ask for complete deletion of your records, or unsubscribe from newsletter communications. To exercise any of these rights, or if you have questions regarding this policy, please reach out to us:
