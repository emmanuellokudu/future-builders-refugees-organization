Our programs

Seven areas of work, planned and carried out with refugees and host community members.

Peacebuilding and social cohesion

FBRO brings together refugees and host community members through peace dialogues, community discussions, sports activities, and awareness campaigns that promote unity and peaceful coexistence.

Livelihood support

We support vulnerable families and youth through poultry keeping, crop farming, and other small income-generating activities that help improve household income and food security.

Youth empowerment and skills development

FBRO supports youth and women through mentorship, leadership activities, vocational skills training, and entrepreneurship awareness programs that help build confidence and self-reliance.

Education and community awareness

We encourage education and learning through mentorship activities, awareness campaigns, and community discussions that promote personal growth and the importance of education.

Environmental conservation

FBRO works with community members to protect the environment through tree planting, environmental awareness activities, and promotion of proper waste management practices.

Community health and wellbeing

We conduct awareness activities on hygiene, sanitation, disease prevention, and healthy living practices to improve community wellbeing.

Cultural and community activities

FBRO promotes positive cultural values, unity, and social inclusion through cultural events and community activities that strengthen relationships among different communities.
